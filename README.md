## Purpose
SUBTRACT ROBOTIX RFD is an emerging independent robotics evaluation and research initiative combining practical field observation with software architecture, AI/ML evaluation, operational analysis, safety considerations, and product-development hypotheses. We aim to help robotics manufacturers, integrators, and buyers understand how robotic systems behave across realistic residential, commercial, indoor, and outdoor applications.

**Status:** Program framework under development. No robotics field tests, manufacturer partnerships, product performance improvements, accreditation, or certifications are claimed by this repository.

## Integrated evaluation domains
- **Field performance:** navigation, indoor/outdoor operation, terrain and surface compatibility.
- **Mechanical systems:** movement, contact, vibration, wear, and maintenance observations.
- **Battery and energy:** task-normalized energy consumption and endurance.
- **AI and software:** autonomy, reasoning behavior, perception, logging, fault recovery, and optimization hypotheses.
- **Safety and usability:** people, property, operator experience, approved environmental limits.
- **Innovation:** accessories, protective contact materials, new use cases, and product improvement concepts.
- **Claims comparison:** documented manufacturer specifications versus measured results under matched conditions.

## Evidence standard
Findings must distinguish **verified**, **conditional**, **inconclusive**, **not supported**, and **not tested**. Every claim needs traceable evidence and limitations. Research hypotheses must not be presented as achieved improvements.

## Selective pilot partnerships
We intend to consider a limited number of manufacturer or research partnerships based on technical fit, authorized equipment access, safety readiness, and the prospect of useful, measurable findings. An initial evaluation can be structured as a bounded pilot with an agreed test plan, report, and equipment-return conditions.

## Independence and safety
Testing requires written authorization, appropriate supervision, manufacturer-approved operating limits, privacy safeguards, and agreed responsibility for equipment and data. This initiative is **not an accredited testing laboratory or certification body**.

## Development roadmap
1. Refine protocols and relevant standards mapping.
2. Establish test instrumentation, forms, and reproducibility checks.
3. Recruit an authorized pilot partner.
4. Perform supervised baseline evaluation.
5. Publish permitted findings and independently validate improvement hypotheses.
