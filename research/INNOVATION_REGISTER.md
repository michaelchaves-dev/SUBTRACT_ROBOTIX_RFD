# Innovation Register

Ideas are **hypotheses, not validated inventions or patent claims**.

| ID | Concept | Motivation | Risks / trade-offs | First validation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RFD-001 | Surface-safe replaceable quadruped foot coverings | Reduce marks, noise, and damage on delicate residential surfaces | Slip, altered gait, heat, joint loads, attachment loss | Prior-art search; manufacturer-approved static material testing; instrumented traction tests |
| RFD-002 | Motion-pattern energy analysis | Identify avoidable stop/start or turn energy cost | Different speeds and terrain affect safety and task completion | Repeat routes, measure Wh/task, control for payload and conditions |
| RFD-003 | Non-safety-critical adaptive inference scheduling | Reduce redundant computation | Latency, perception degradation, safety dependency | Offline profiling before any controlled integration |
| RFD-004 | Cross-domain residential readiness checklist | Connect surface, safety, usability and environmental observations | False assurance if incomplete | Map documented claims and limitations to test scenarios |

**For each idea:** prior art -> hypothesis -> risk assessment -> controlled test -> evidence -> independent review -> go/no-go.
