# Pilot Access, Safety and Ethics

**Precondition:** Written authorization from equipment owner and a designated safety lead. This project is not an accredited certification body or safety laboratory.

## Before testing
- Define model, firmware, payload, location, permitted use, manufacturer environmental ratings and operating limits.
- Confirm competent supervision, operator training, emergency-stop procedures, exclusion zones and incident response.
- Agree custody, transport, insurance, property damage, confidentiality, IP, data/video rights, publication permissions and return arrangements.
- Identify relevant standards with qualified engineering review; standards applicability varies by robot and application.
- Obtain informed permission for any recorded people or private premises; minimize personal data.

## Prohibited in unsupervised pilot
No deliberate collision with people or animals; no unauthorized rain/immersion, contaminants, destructive loading, electrical or battery abuse, safety-system bypass, or operation outside approved specifications. Use simulated obstacles and safe surrogates first.

## Suggested limited pilot
One authorized platform; one agreed environment; defined operating window; three to five repeatable tasks; baseline telemetry; documented findings and prioritized follow-up experiments. No guarantee of improvement or certification. Free initial pilot, if offered, must have bounded scope and clear written terms.

## Go/no-go
Stop for unanticipated hazard, damage, unstable control, loss of communications, battery alarm, weather outside rating, or unapproved bystander access.
