# Announcement Video — Three 10-Second Slices

**Use:** Three separate 10-second video generations, assembled in order into a 30-second concept explainer. **Format:** 9:16 vertical, cinematic realistic, restrained graphite/steel-blue palette, no invented company logo. All robot footage must be clearly labeled **CONCEPT VISUALIZATION — NOT ACTUAL SAS TESTING**. No claims of completed trials.

## Slice 1 (00:00–00:10): The real world is the test
**Video prompt:** Photorealistic cinematic montage of a generic unbranded quadruped robot, first walking through a clean industrial lab, then transitioning into a realistic residential hallway with polished flooring and door thresholds, finally approaching an outdoor path after rain. No humans in danger, no collisions, no logos. Slow controlled camera tracking, high-end engineering documentary aesthetic. On-screen title: “BEYOND THE SPECIFICATIONS.” Small persistent label: “CONCEPT VISUALIZATION”.
**Voiceover:** “A robot's specifications tell one story. Real environments reveal another.”

## Slice 2 (00:10–00:20): One test, multiple disciplines
**Video prompt:** Generic unbranded quadruped robot performing a safe, supervised controlled walking trial on dry flooring. Elegant non-fiction-style overlays show battery energy, foot contact, navigation path, and software diagnostics. Close-up of replaceable contact pad concept, clearly speculative. Camera moves between mechanical detail and a realistic telemetry workstation. No fabricated numerical readings. On-screen title: “ONE PLATFORM. MULTIPLE PERSPECTIVES.” Small label: “CONCEPT VISUALIZATION”.
**Voiceover:** “We connect mechanical performance, energy, software, safety, and product innovation.”

## Slice 3 (00:20–00:30): The offer
**Video prompt:** Cinematic close-up of a professional evaluation report on a workstation with abstract charts, engineering notes and a generic robot in the background. Camera pulls back to a premium minimal black slate. Accurate large typography: “SUBTRACT ROBOTIX RFD” then “Research • Field Evaluation • Development” and “A Subtract Architect Studios initiative.” Small label: “CONCEPT VISUALIZATION”. No client logos or endorsement claims.
**Voiceover:** “Subtract Robotix RFD. Integrated robotics intelligence. Beyond the specifications.”

## One-page visual brief
Headline: **SUBTRACT ROBOTIX RFD**. Subhead: **Independent Robotics Evaluation & Optimization**. Promise: **One platform. Multiple perspectives. Actionable intelligence.** Sections: Real-world testing; cross-disciplinary analysis; evidence-backed recommendations; selective pilot partnerships. Footer: **A Subtract Architect Studios initiative**. Include a visible “Proposed program — pilot partners sought” qualifier until testing is established.
